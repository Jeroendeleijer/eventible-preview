About this event

A four-evening course for people who want to make something that lasts longer than a taster session. Week one we pinch and coil, week two we slab-build, week three we trim and refine, week four we glaze. Everything is fired between sessions. Six people, no more.

What's included

All clay, firing, glazes and tools

What to bring

An apron if you have one

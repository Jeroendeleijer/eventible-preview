# Seed photo credits

Cover photographs used by the demo data in `app/seed.py` and on the landing
page, resized, stripped of EXIF and converted to WebP. Most come from Wikimedia
Commons via `tools/fetch_seed_photos.py`; the rows marked *Supplied* were
brought in with `tools/import_photo.py` and carry no Commons licence — clear
their rights with whoever supplied them before launch.

**These are placeholders. Replace them with your own photography before
launch.** The attribution below is a licence requirement for everything that
is not public domain, and must be kept if the images are redistributed. A
CC BY-SA licence is *share-alike*: modifying and redistributing the image
itself obliges you to license the result the same way. It has no effect on the
rest of the codebase.

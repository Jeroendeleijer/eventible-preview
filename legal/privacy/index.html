Privacy policy

This is the outline the platform is built against, not finished legal copy. It should be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

Where your data lives

All infrastructure and every third-party processor — application hosting, database, file and image storage, backups, email, moderation APIs, analytics, error tracking and logging — runs in EU regions. Personal data does not leave the EU. Vendors without an EU region are excluded. Payment processing runs through Stripe's European entity.

What we keep and for how long

Orders are retained for 7 years for tax purposes. Inactive accounts are anonymised after 3 years. Original uploaded images are retained privately as moderation evidence and are never served publicly.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data. Organisers who export attendee lists do so under a data processing agreement with Eventible.

Processors

Stripe (payments, identity), an image moderation API, a CSAM hash-matching service, a transactional email provider, maps and geocoding, and product analytics. The current list is reviewed before any new vendor is added.

Terms of service

This is the outline the platform is built against, not finished legal copy. It should be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

Who sells the ticket

The organiser is the seller of the ticket and is responsible for the event. Eventible is the marketplace and payment facilitator.

Age

You must be 18 or older to hold an account. Events open to children are booked by a parent or guardian.

Refunds

Organisers set their own refund policy from the presets we offer. An organiser cancellation, or a material change to the date or location, always gives a full refund regardless of that policy.

Fees

Eventible charges a fixed fee when an event is published and a handling fee on every ticket sold. Both are shown to the organiser before publishing. Drafts are free, and free tickets carry no handling fee.

Content and conduct

Every event passes an automated legality and image check. Breaches follow a strike system: warning, a seven-day publishing suspension, then a permanent ban. Illegal content results in an immediate ban.
